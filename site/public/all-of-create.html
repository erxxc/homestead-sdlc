Before joining

Use pack version 2.6

Install the exact All of Create 2.6 client from CurseForge and allocate at least 6 GB of memory. Other pack versions cannot join.

Progression

Build the grid

Create machinery leads into Power Grid, Electro Energetics, and the quested Create: New Age reactor path. Nuclear power is the late-game target.

Operations

Rollback stays available

The world, runtime, integrity baseline, and backups are isolated. Homestead 2.0.1 remains retained as the primary-slot rollback release.

Fresh primary world

No prior world was converted.

All of Create starts from its own world. The Homestead 2.0.1 and 1.3.6 worlds remain under their original releases and are never opened by NeoForge.
